Other Certifications

PRINCE2 Foundation & Practitioner

PRojects IN Controlled Environments | December 2010

+

Dual-level certification in PRINCE2—the globally recognized, process-based project management methodology originally developed by the UK Government's Central Computer and Telecommunications Agency (CCTA), later the Office of Government Commerce (OGC), and now maintained by AXELOS. PRINCE2 provides a structured, scalable framework for managing projects of any size, complexity, or industry sector within controlled environments.

Foundation Level validates comprehensive understanding of the PRINCE2 methodology, including mastery of its core framework: the 7 Principles (continued business justification, learn from experience, defined roles and responsibilities, manage by stages, manage by exception, focus on products, and tailor to suit the project environment), the 7 Themes (business case, organization, quality, plans, risk, change, and progress), and the 7 Processes (starting up a project, directing a project, initiating a project, controlling a stage, managing product delivery, managing stage boundaries, and closing a project). Proficient in understanding the inputs, outputs, and management products across the project lifecycle.

Practitioner Level demonstrates the ability to apply and tailor PRINCE2 methodology to real-world project scenarios. Expertise in adapting the framework to suit specific project environments while maintaining the integrity of its principles. Skilled at implementing stage-gate controls, managing by exception through defined tolerances, maintaining continuous business justification, and ensuring product-focused delivery. Proficient in establishing appropriate governance structures, defining clear accountability through the project board, and managing risk and change within controlled parameters. Capable of integrating lessons learned throughout the project lifecycle and ensuring alignment between project objectives and organizational strategy.
